Karma, too, is of two kinds: Pravritta karma, done under personal desire for gain, and Nivritta karma, done without such desire, under the guidance of perfected knowledge — and that rises beyond the five elements. The Bhagavad Gita closes the chapter: worship Him in your own karma, and you shall win perfection; better your own dharma, though destitute of merits, than another's well executed. "Ishvara dwelleth in the hearts of all beings, O Arjuna. Flee unto Him for shelter with all thy being; by His grace thou shalt obtain supreme peace, the everlasting dwelling place."
