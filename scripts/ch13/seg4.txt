In the early days, the book says, the outer caste coincided with the stage, and society was contented and progressive. But in these later days, varnasankara, caste-confusion, has come, as Arjuna truly foresaw in his distress on Kurukshetra. Young souls are born into bodies of other stages, and older souls fret against uncongenial surroundings. The evil has grown from each caste grasping at the work of the others, and thinking more of the rights its caste gives than of the duties it imposes, until antagonisms have replaced mutual service and good will. Yet the Rigveda says: the Brahmana was His mouth; the Rajanya was made His two arms; His two thighs the Vaishya; the Shudra was born from His two feet, one body, four parts.
