Chapter Thirteen. The Four Castes. This chapter of Sanatana Dharma, the 1916 textbook of the Central Hindu College, turns from the soul's journey to the shape of society. In the soul's long pilgrimage through many births, the book says, there are four distinctly marked stages, called of old varnas, or colours. And Sanatana Dharma made those four stages the framework of its social polity.
