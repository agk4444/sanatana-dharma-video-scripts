We have seen that there is a law of Yajna, of sacrifice, and that man's glory is to work with it willingly. Now the book sets out the five great sacrifices to be offered every day. The first is the sacrifice to the Rishis, the sages, or to the Vedas: study and teaching. Every day a man should study some sacred book, a few verses of the Bhagavad Gita or the Hamsa Gita, thinking them over carefully, for carefulness matters more than the amount read. And he should be ready to share what he learns with those more ignorant than himself. All study, the book says, should be a sacrifice, learning in order that we may teach.
