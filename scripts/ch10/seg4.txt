The third is the sacrifice to the Pitris, the ancestors, the offering of water, Tarpana, to the older generations of our family. Its inner meaning is the great debt we owe to the past, to those who toiled and laboured to hand the earth on, improved and enriched, to us, their posterity. No man is truly human, the book says, who does not recognise what he owes to the past. The fourth is the sacrifice to men: hospitality. Every day a true Aryan should feed someone poorer than himself. Its inner meaning is the duty of serving humanity, feeding the hungry, clothing the naked, sheltering the homeless, comforting the sad. Those who are rich are the stewards of the poor.
