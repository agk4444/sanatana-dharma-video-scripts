The control of speech is the next step: we must think before we speak, for hasty speech leads to endless troubles. Arjuna was often very hasty in his speech, and so got himself into many difficulties, as in his vow to kill Jayadratha before sunset, which needed Shri Krishna to shroud the sun. His failure to keep a hasty vow led to his death on the Great Journey. He who has conquered his tongue is near to self-mastery. Thirdly, the physical body must be controlled. Shri Krishna calls the austerity of the body the worship of the Devas, the twice-born, the gurus and the wise, purity, straightforwardness, chastity and harmlessness. Youth is the time for conquering the body, for the body is a creature of habit, and a little perseverance makes it give way.
