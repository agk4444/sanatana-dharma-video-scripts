We have seen that the Jivatma is related to all around him, but he is most closely related to his own bodies, his koshas, the parts of the Not-Self he has most closely united to himself. Unless he is in harmonious relations with these, he can hardly hope to be in harmonious relations with anything further away. While he is young, these bodies master him and lead him into trouble. As he grows older, he begins to master them, and many a hard fight he has before he develops the great virtue called self-control: the control of the lower selves by the higher Self, of the bodies by the Jivatma.
