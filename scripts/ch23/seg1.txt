Chapter Twenty-Three. Virtues and Vices in relation to Inferiors. As we go further into the world, says Sanatana Dharma, the 1916 textbook of the Central Hindu College, we meet many people below us in some way: younger than ourselves, less educated, poorer, or lower in social rank. What virtues should we cultivate toward them, and what vices should we avoid, if our relations with them are to be harmonious?
