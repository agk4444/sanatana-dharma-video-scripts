The chapter quotes the Katha Upanishad: the Self-born pierced the senses outwards, therefore the Jiva looks outwards, away from the inner Self. And the Chandogya Upanishad: when the Jiva obtains pleasure, then he engages in action; never does he enter on action without having obtained pleasure. But the book draws a sharp line. That which is infinite, that is happiness; there is no happiness in the finite. Where the Self sees no other than the Self, that is the Infinite; where it sees another, that is the finite. The infinite is immortal; the finite is mortal.
