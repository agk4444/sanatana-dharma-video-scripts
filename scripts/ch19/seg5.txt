As a man cultivates love, he unites himself to an ever-widening circle: the family, the community, the nation, the race, loving them as himself. That love is the Bliss in his nature finding satisfaction in union. Evolution itself is leading us towards union, the will of Ishvara guiding the separated selves towards union with each other and with Himself. In this union is Bliss; therefore the Right and the Happy are inseparable. Brahman is bliss. Next: Chapter 20 — Self-regarding Virtues.
