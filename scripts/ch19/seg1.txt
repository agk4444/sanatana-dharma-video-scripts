Chapter Nineteen. Bliss and Emotions. In Part Three, Ethical Teachings, of Sanatana Dharma, the 1916 textbook of the Central Hindu College, the book turns from what man is to how he should live, and it begins with bliss, the third aspect of Ishvara himself. For man, as the child of Ishvara, carries Thought, Power and Bliss within him, and this chapter asks where bliss is to be found.
