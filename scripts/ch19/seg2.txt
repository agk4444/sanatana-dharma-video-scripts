Ishvara is Thought, Power and Bliss, and man, as His child, shares those three. But when the Jivatma, the separated Self, is wrapped in dense matter, its Bliss turns outward, seeking satisfaction by uniting itself with the outer world. The impulses outward are called desires. When desire unites the Jivatma to an object that gives pleasure, so that he wishes to be united to it again, the emotion is love, or liking. When it unites him to an object that gives pain, so that he wishes to avoid it, the emotion is hate, or dislike. Love is attraction; hate is repulsion.
