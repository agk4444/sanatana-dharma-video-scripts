Brahman has two states. Unmanifested, without attributes, He is called Nirguna Brahman. Manifested, with attributes, He is Saguna Brahman, the Supreme Ishvara, with His universe. With Himself as Spirit, Ishvara reveals the other side of the All: Mulaprakriti, the Root of Matter. Matter is that which takes form. Everything we can touch, taste, smell, see and hear is Matter. But the whole of it is not Matter. In each thing, inaudible and invisible, there is Spirit, a portion of Ishvara. The Matter part is called the body, the sheath, the vehicle. The Spirit in a body is called the Jiva, a separated Self.
