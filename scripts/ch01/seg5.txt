What makes Matter begin to take form is Shakti, the Divine Power of Ishvara, called Maya. Ishvara is the Lord of Maya. And the chapter closes with the words of the Bhagavad Gita: Brahman is everywhere, with hands and feet everywhere, eyes, heads and mouths everywhere, unattached, supporting everything, seated in the hearts of all. That is the One Existence. And from it, the book will go on to the Many, to rebirth, to karma, and to sacrifice.
