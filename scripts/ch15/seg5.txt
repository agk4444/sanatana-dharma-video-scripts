Boys, the book says, must at first take moral precepts on the authority of great Sages and Saints, as taught in the Shastras, because they have not the power nor the time to think them out for themselves. But in manhood they can verify these precepts by applying their Reason to them. And the chapter closes with the words of Shri Krishna: "I am the Self, O Gudakesha, seated in the heart of all beings; I am the beginning, the middle, and also the end of beings." Let this principle sink deeply into every heart. Next: Chapter 16 — Right and Wrong.
