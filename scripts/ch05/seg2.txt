The first thing to grasp is that creation is sacrifice. Ishvara confines Himself, limits Himself, in matter, in order that a universe may be made manifest. Immersion in matter is, in spiritual language, called death, and Ishvara thus sacrificed Himself to bring into separate being portions of Himself, the Jivas, who might develop all His powers in matter. This is the primary sacrifice, and on this is based the Law. Sacrifice, then, is the pouring out of life for the benefit of others.
