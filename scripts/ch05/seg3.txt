The Law of Sacrifice is the law of life for all Jivas. In the earlier stages they are forcibly sacrificed, and so progress involuntarily. The mineral is broken up for the support of plants, the vegetable for the support of animal life, the animal for the support of other animals and of men. Only after untold ages does the Jiva recognise the universality of the law, and begin to sacrifice deliberately for the good of those around him. This is called self-sacrifice, and it is the showing forth of the divinity of the Jiva, the proof that he is of the nature of Ishvara.
