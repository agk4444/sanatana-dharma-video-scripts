Another lesson of these sacrifices is that all lives are inter-dependent. The Rishis taught man to sacrifice daily to the Devas, to Rishis, to ancestors, to men and animals, for as all these enrich his life, he owes them a debt, which he must pay by sacrifice. As he lives on others, he must, in common honesty, live for others. At last sacrifice becomes happiness, a delight. He tries to see how little he can take and how much he can give, and to become the friend of all creatures. Then every action becomes a sacrifice to Ishvara, and actions no longer bind him. Thus the law of sacrifice becomes also the law of liberation. Next: Chapter 6 — The Worlds, Visible and Invisible.
