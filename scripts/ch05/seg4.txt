The Mahabharata tells a wonderful story of uttermost self-sacrifice. Indra, king of the Devas, was defeated by the Asura Vritra, and could only be saved by a thunderbolt made from the self-given bones of a holy one. The Rishi Dadhichi was filled with great pity and said, "I give to you my body willingly, to make what use of it you like." His bones were taken, and Vritra fell before the might born of that wondrous sacrifice. The Rishis lead the Jiva to this point step by step, teaching him first to sacrifice his possessions, and to deny himself present enjoyments, in the hope of reward, until the practice of sacrificing becomes habitual.
