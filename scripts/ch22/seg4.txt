Brotherly love is shown in the Ramayana, where Lakshmana is like Rama's life itself: they would not sleep apart, nor apart engage in sport, and Lakshmana follows Rama into the forest and stands waking on watch while Rama sleeps. When Lakshmana lies senseless, arrow-pierced, Rama cries, without thee, life and victory, nay, Sita's self, are worthless. The Pandavas show the same union: never with warring interests, never leading separated lives, all their wealth gained for Yudhishthira, and he regarding their joys and sorrows as his own. Even in heaven, Yudhishthira will not stay without his brothers, crying, that is heaven where my brothers are; this is not heaven for me. For stronger than hell is love, and fidelity than pain.
