Chapter Twenty-Two. Virtues and Vices in relation to Equals. This chapter of Sanatana Dharma, the 1916 textbook of the Central Hindu College, turns from our duties to those above us, to the equals who surround us on every side: in the home, and in the world outside it. What virtues should we develop, and what vices avoid, to make our home and our outer relations harmonious and happy?
