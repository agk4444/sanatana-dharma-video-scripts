By showing love to others, we awaken love in them; by showing hate, we awaken hate. An angry word brings an angry reply, and that brings a still more angry retort. Gentle words bring gentle words in reply; kindness arouses kindness, and good deeds cause good deeds in others. So we can meet a wrong feeling with a right one, instead of being swept into it. When Draupadi urged King Yudhishthira to attack the Kurus who had cheated and ruined him, the wise king answered that returning evil for evil could only continue misery. If the injured returned their injuries, he said, fathers would slay sons and sons fathers, and how could birth take place in a world filled with anger? For the birth of creatures is due to peace.
