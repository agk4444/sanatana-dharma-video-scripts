But the same law works for evil. Thoughtless ridicule arouses hatred: when Bhima laughed at Duryodhana slipping in Yudhishthira's hall, the insult festered into vengeance, the gambling match, the exile, and at last the battle of Kurukshetra. Evil returned with evil only lengthens the chain of misery. In anger, Jamadagni ordered his son Rama of the Axe to slay his own mother, and that deed led to murder upon murder, until Rama vowed the slaughter of a whole caste. Only forgiveness can cut such a chain. Even Duryodhana was once softened: treated with unfailing patience by his difficult guest Durvasa, he won a boon. Yet when Yudhishthira later rescued him from his enemies, the very kindness became a new offence, and he hated only the more.
