So the book ends with practice, not theory. Even when another's anger arouses anger in us, we may check it and refuse it expression in word, look, or gesture, until the feeling dies away. Avoid bad company, whose unkind thoughts work upon us, and keep the mind busy with pure and high thoughts, so we influence others for good. For he who is not angry with the angry is a physician unto both, and this world is upheld by forgiveness. May all rejoice everywhere. Peace to all beings.
