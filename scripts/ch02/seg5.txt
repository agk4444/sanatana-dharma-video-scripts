The chapter closes with the words of the sages. The Rigveda says: Of Him who is one, sages speak as manifold. And the Upanishad gives the image of a blazing fire, from which sparks, all similar to each other, spring forth in thousands, so from the Indestructible, various types of being are born, and they also return thither. And the Bhagavad Gita promises: When dharma decays, when adharma is exalted, then I Myself come forth, for the protection of the good, for firmly establishing dharma, I am born from age to age.
