Brahma then created in His mind minerals, plants, animals and men, and this world-evolution, called Samsara, is compared to a wheel, constantly turning, on which all Jivas are bound. Vishnu breathed His Life into all these forms and became Prana in all forms, and the whole universe became full of life and consciousness. But the life of Mahadeva must also be poured out, to complete the triple Jiva of man, that he might be the perfect reflection of the triple Ishvara. And Vishnu comes down again and again as an Avatara, One who descends, whenever things are going badly with the world. The Ten Avataras mark the great stages of evolution: Matsya the Fish, Kurma the Tortoise, Varaha the Boar, Narasimha the Man-Lion, Vamana the Dwarf, Parashurama, Rama the Perfect Man, Krishna, Buddha, and Kalki, who shall close the Kali Yuga.
