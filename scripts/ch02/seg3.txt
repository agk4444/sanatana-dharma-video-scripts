In everything there are three gunas, three qualities, never separated, though one may dominate. When Sattva dominates, the being is called sattvik; when Rajas dominates, rajasik; when Tamas dominates, tamasik. The Elements are mostly tamasik, dull and inert. The ten Indriyas, the five senses and the five powers of action, with Manah, the mind, are mostly rajasik, very active. The Devas, the ministers of Ishvara who administer His laws and see that each man gets what he has earned by his karma, are mostly sattvik. Ishvara is the King, the One Lord, and the Devas are His ministers, like the ministers and officials of an earthly king.
