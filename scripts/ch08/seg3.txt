Why does this matter? So long as the body remains undissolved, the Pranamayakosha hangs round it, held by a magnetic attraction, and the Jiva is thus linked to the earth, which is bad for him. A slowly decaying body in the ground, as in burial, sends out poisonous gases, which is bad for those left behind. The Shraddhas performed after the burning help in a re-arrangement of the materials of the Manomayakosha, the mind-sheath, partly by the magnetic influences of the objects used, and partly by the vibrations set up by the mantras. After a certain time comes the Sapindikarana ceremony, which helps the Jiva to pass from Pretaloka, the world of the Preta, to Pitriloka, the world of the ancestors, where he is enrolled among the Pitris.
