Seven generations, the book says, one in Bhurloka, our world, and six in Bhuvarloka, can affect each other. The living help the dead, and the dead, as Pitris, watch over the living. Shraddha, the book defines it, is this: gifts to deserving Brahmanas for the sake of benefit to the Pitris, in the proper times and places and with faith, are known as Shraddha. It should be offered to the Pitris day by day, with corn and water, or even roots and water, bringing them satisfaction. And when the Jiva passes on into Svarga, heaven, he has no further need of the help that Shraddha gives.
