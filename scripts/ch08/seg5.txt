The book also speaks of the soul's own road after death. From the five elements a subtler but firm body is born for sinful souls, that they may suffer the punishments awarded by Yama; afterwards it dissolves again into the elements. But if a man has done more Dharma and little Adharma, he goes on to Svarga and enjoys happiness there. The Jiva remains on the path for a year, and by the offering of the sixteen Shraddhas he is helped to dwell in joy with the Pitris. Therefore should the son ever perform the rites for his father.
