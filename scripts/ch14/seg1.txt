Chapter Fourteen. Ethical Science, what it is. Part Three of Sanatana Dharma, the 1916 textbook of the Central Hindu College, opens its Ethical Teachings with a plain question: what makes a list of rules into a science? The chapter answers by defining its two words — science, and ethical — and from those two definitions it builds a whole teaching about right conduct, about Dharma, and about the happiness that right conduct brings.
