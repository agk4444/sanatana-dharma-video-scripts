The fruit of wrongdoing may taste sweet, but it is poison, bringing pain afterwards. The boy who does wrong is like the ignorant child who plucks and eats the poison-berries, and writhes in pain in the evening. The teacher of morality must mark every sin Poison. Then comes Achara, good conduct. Achara is the mark of Dharma, higher than all teachings is Achara, the Mahabharata says. From Achara is Dharma born, and Dharma enhances life. Dharma is defined by what it does: it upholds and holds together; it brings the well-being of all beings; it makes beings harmless. He who is the friend of all beings, intent on the welfare of all in act, thought and speech — he knoweth Dharma.
