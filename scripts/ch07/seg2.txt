Every religion prescribes ceremonies, and the book gives them three uses. First, to help the Jiva conquer and purify his sheaths. Second, to bring to his aid superior Intelligences, such as the Devas and the Rishis. And third, to improve the atmosphere around him, so that he can steady and concentrate his mind. To this end are used material objects, gestures, postures and sounds, all carefully arranged. The objects are chosen for their good magnetism. A rosary of tulasi beads is used in the worship of Shri Krishna, and one of rudraksha in the worship of Mahadeva, because each shares the magnetism of the object of worship, and makes a link between that object and the worshipper.
