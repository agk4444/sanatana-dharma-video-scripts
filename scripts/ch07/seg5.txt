Among the ceremonies in the life of a Hindu are some that mark the chief stages of his life. In old days ten stood out as the chief. Seven belong to infancy and early childhood; of these the first feeding with solid food, Annaprashanam, is universally observed, and the tonsure with the piercing of the ears, Chudakaranam, almost universally. Then comes the eighth, the Upanayana, when the boy is led to the Guru, who gives him the sacred thread and teaches him the Gayatri, making him a Dvija, twice-born. The student stage follows, closed by the return home, the Samavartanam, and then marriage, Vivaha, marks his entry into the householder life. May the ancient rule be speedily revived.
