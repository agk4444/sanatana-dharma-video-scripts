There is another thing to remember about mantra. If the man who utters it is not trying to lead a good life, the vibrations of the mantra will do him harm, not good, for they work on his subtle body and shake it violently. If his resolves are good, however weak they may be, they work with the mantra and cause no conflict. And mantras need not be spoken aloud. Their silent repetition is, indeed, more powerful than audible recitation, for they affect the subtler kinds of matter more than the denser. The book gives the saying: one sound, well-uttered, becometh as the cow of plenty in the Svarga-world.
