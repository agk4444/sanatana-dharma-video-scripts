Let the young student grasp firmly this distinction between Right and Wrong, the book says, and apply it to the guidance of his conduct. In later study he will add much to his thoughts on Right and Wrong, will learn many details, and have to solve many complexities. But this principle, this standard, will remain unaltered, for it is based on evolution, and is in accord with the will of Ishvara. The chapter then quotes the Manusmriti: of all these things, the knowledge of the Self is the highest. It is the foremost of the sciences, for immortality is attained thereby. He who thus seeth the Self in all beings, by his own Self, realiseth the equality of all, and attaineth to the supreme state of Brahman.
