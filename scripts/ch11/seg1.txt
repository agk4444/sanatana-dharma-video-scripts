Chapter Eleven. Worship. The fifth chapter of Part Two of Sanatana Dharma, the 1916 textbook of the Central Hindu College, turns from duty to devotion. The five daily sacrifices are not enough, the book says. The truly religious man longs for something more: to come into conscious relation with Ishvara, the Lord of the Universe, the great Life of which his own life is a part. That longing finds its satisfaction in worship.
