Even Vyasa, the book tells us, knew this longing. Master of the final truth, he had written the Mahabharata and the Brahma-sutras for the instruction of men, yet still he found no peace of mind. Narada counselled him to sing the praise of Ishvara, and this he did in the Bhagavata, winning thereby the peace he had not gained before. Worship, the book says, is the expression of love to the Supreme, of reverence towards Him, of aspiration to reach Him in conscious communion, of longing to be united with Him, to feel the unity of the individual self with the supreme Self.
