Upasana includes many forms of worship, among them meditation and the daily Sandhya, which should be performed by all followers of the Sanatana Dharma. And the chapter closes with the words of the Bhagavad Gita: "However men approach Me, even so do I welcome them, for the path men take from every side is mine." And again: "The Lord dwelleth in the hearts of all beings. Flee unto Him for shelter with all thy being; by His grace thou shalt obtain supreme peace and the everlasting dwelling place."
