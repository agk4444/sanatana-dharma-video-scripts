When the householder sees his sons able to bear his duties, when the signs of age appear and his children's children are round him, the time has come to retire with his wife to a quiet, secluded life of study and counselling the younger. Finally, in old age, a man may enter the fourth Ashrama, passing his last days in meditation and worship, till he enters willingly the life beyond death. The book closes with the words of the Gita: he who performs his duty, independent of the fruit of action, he is a Sannyasi, he is a Yogi. Next: Chapter 13 — The Four Castes.
