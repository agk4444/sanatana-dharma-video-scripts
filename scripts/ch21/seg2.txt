Love toward God shows itself as reverence, devotion, worship, and submission to His will. Bhishma, at the Rajasuya sacrifice, bids them offer the first worship to Shri Krishna, and dying, he thinks of Krishna in mind, word, and act, closing with the thousand names of Vasudeva. Prahlada prays to Hari though his father threatens to kill him, and Narasimha bursts from the pillar to save him. Dhruva worships with such fervour that Hari gives him the pole-star for a throne. And Rama, excluded from the throne, stays utterly unshaken, knowing the Real amid changing unrealities.
