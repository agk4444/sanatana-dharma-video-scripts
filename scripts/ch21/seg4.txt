The King, the book says, exists to uphold righteousness, not to act capriciously. All creatures rest upon righteousness, and righteousness rests upon the King. Patriotism and public spirit belong with loyalty, for King and Country are one object of love. Public spirit makes a man feel the successes and sufferings of his country as his own, protect the weak, uphold the law, and refuse unfair profit at the community's cost. To parents is due the most complete obedience. Rama, told of his exile, answers at once that he will carry out his father's word, saying there is no power in him to transgress his father's order, and later, what my father hath commanded must not be made untrue.
