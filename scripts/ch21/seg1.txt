Chapter Twenty-One. Virtues and Vices in relation to Superiors. This chapter of Sanatana Dharma, the 1916 textbook of the Central Hindu College, turns from ethics in general to how love should shape our conduct toward those above us. Love, the book says, is the root of the virtues, for it prompts us to sacrifice for others, while hate is the root of the vices, for it grasps at things for ourselves alone. Our superiors are five: God, the Sovereign, our Parents, our Teachers, and the Aged.
