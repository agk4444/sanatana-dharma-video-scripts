Those who set themselves against the Lord fall. Ravana, monarch of Lanka, fell because he thought himself a rival of Ishvara. Jarasandha was slain by Bhima, Shishupala before the discus of the Lord he denied, and Duryodhana perished for rejecting Shri Krishna's counsels. Loyalty to the head of the State is insisted on just as strongly. The Pandavas bring all the wealth they win in war to Yudhishthira's feet, fighting for their King, not for themselves. And when the people follow the exiled Yudhishthira, he bids them return and obey their proper ruler, since only thus can they secure the general prosperity.
