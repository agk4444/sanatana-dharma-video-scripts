Whatever we sow by our actions comes back to us. If we make the people around us happy, we have sown happiness, and it grows up into happiness for ourselves. If we make them unhappy, we have sown unhappiness, and it returns as unhappiness for ourselves. But thought stands behind action, and thought makes our character. As we think, so we become: think kindly and we become kind; think cruelly and we become cruel. And when we are born again, we shall be born with the character our thoughts are making now. Behind thought stands desire. As a magnet attracts soft iron, so desire attracts objects: desire learning, and we shall have the chance to become learned in another life; desire love, and the chance of becoming loved.
