The chapter closes with the old scriptures. Man verily is desire-formed, says the Brihadaranyaka Upanishad: as is his desire, so goes his thought; as his thought is, so he does action; as he does action, so he attains. And the Katha Upanishad gives the image of the chariot: the Self is the chariot-owner, Reason the charioteer, the mind the reins, the senses the horses. With mind tightened, the senses are controlled, like good horses of the charioteer. That is karma: we reap as we sow, and we are free to sow anew. Next: Chapter 5 — Sacrifice.
