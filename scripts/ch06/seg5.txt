At death, the subtle body separates from the solid body, and the Jiva sheds its sheaths one by one, becoming a Preta, then a purified Pitri, then entering Svarga to enjoy the fruit it has stored up. When that fruit is exhausted, the Devas build for it new sheaths, according to its karma, and it is again born into Bhurloka. Life, death, life in the invisible worlds, re-birth, repeated time after time, until at last the Jiva grows weary of these three worlds, turns to meditation and worship and the compassionate helping of the weaker, and becomes a co-worker with Ishvara.
