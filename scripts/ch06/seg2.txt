We all know one world, the world around us, which we can see and hear and touch and taste and smell. But science itself tells us of parts of this world our senses are too dull to perceive, things too small for our eyes to see. These parts are still physical, although invisible to us. And beyond them, the book says, there are other worlds, invisible and not a part of this one: the worlds into which people go when they pass away from this earth by death. These are the Triloka, the three worlds.
