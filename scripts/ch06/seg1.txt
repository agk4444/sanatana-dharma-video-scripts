Chapter Six. The Worlds — Visible and Invisible. The sixth chapter of Sanatana Dharma, the 1916 textbook of the Central Hindu College, turns from the One Existence to the worlds the Jiva travels through on its long pilgrimage. We know the world our senses show us. But the book says there are other worlds too, invisible ones, where the Jiva goes after death, and in these three worlds its evolution proceeds.
