The three great lokas are Bhurloka, the physical world, the earth; Bhuvarloka, the world of becoming, intermediate between the earth and heaven; and Svargaloka, the heaven world. They are made at the beginning of an immense period called a Day of Brahma, and perish at its ending. In Bhurloka, the Prithvi Tattva is the basis of all forms, in seven modifications: solid, liquid, gaseous, radiant, etheric, superetheric, and atomic. In Bhuvarloka the basis is the Apas Tattva, and in Svargaloka the Agni Tattva.
