The student may ask: what makes the forms evolve to suit the Jiva? The Jiva's own efforts. He wants to look out through the wall of matter that encloses him. He tries to see, and his outward-going energy slowly, very slowly, evolves an eye. The senses, the book says, are pierced from within outwards. The Devas help him, giving him materials suited to the organ he wants: Agni gives fiery matter for sight, Varuna watery matter for taste. When he has shaped a body as much as he can, and it is of no further use to him, he throws it away and makes another, and he evolves faster and faster as he goes on.
