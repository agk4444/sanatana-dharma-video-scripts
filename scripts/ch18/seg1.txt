Chapter Eighteen. Virtues and their foundation. In Part Three of Sanatana Dharma, the 1916 textbook of the Central Hindu College, the book asks what a virtue really is, and where virtues come from. We have already seen, it says, that mutual sacrifice, another name for mutual service, is enjoined by the Sanatana Dharma, because it promotes union. And there is another way the Dharma teaches us to do right: by explaining the three debts that we owe.
