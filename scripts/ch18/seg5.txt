Karna gave away the natural armour no weapon could pierce, hewing it off with his own hand, though he knew it meant his life. King Dasharatha kept the two boons he had promised Kaikeyi, though granting them meant his death. And Bali gave Vamana the land of three paces, and offered his own head for the third, though he knew Vamana was Vishnu, his enemy. Above and beyond all else, therefore, a boy should strive for truthfulness. For, as the book says, Truth is the Sanatana Dharma. Truth is the eternal Brahman. Next: Chapter 19 — Bliss and Emotions.
