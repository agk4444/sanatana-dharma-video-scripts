The three debts are these. The debt to the Rishis, paid chiefly in the Brahmacharya Ashrama, by study and by service of the teacher. The debt to the Pitris, the Ancestors, paid chiefly in the Garhasthya Ashrama, by rearing a family and by charity. And the debt to the Devas, paid chiefly in the Vanaprastha Ashrama, by sacrifice and meditation. A debt is something we owe: we have received, and should repay. This payment of what is due is called the discharge of duty. The discharge of a duty is called a virtue; the non-discharge of a duty is called a vice. A virtuous man recognises his duties and fulfils them; a vicious man does not recognise them, nor fulfil them.
