The food we eat must be pure. Fresh leaves and fruits, grains and roots are full of life that is unfolding, and they build the body up; they become impure only when they are stale. All flesh is more or less impure, because its indwelling life has been driven from it and it is ready to decay; a body built on it is more liable to disease, wounds heal less quickly, and fevers run higher. Pure water is necessary to health, and milk is food and drink of the purest kind. But every drink into which alcohol enters is impure, a poison to the brain — and in a hot climate it brings premature decay and early death.
