Shaucha, or Shaucham, is the set of rules for keeping the body pure, and so keeping it healthy and strong. Disease, the book says, is a sign that some law of nature has been disregarded, and the laws of nature are the laws of God. The body is built from the food we eat, the liquids we drink, the air we breathe — and from a constant rain of minute particles falling on us from the people and things around us. Everything we touch gives us some of its particles, and we give it some of ours. So we must take in only pure particles, and drive away the impure ones.
